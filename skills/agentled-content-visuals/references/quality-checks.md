# Visual quality checks

- Does the visual support the specific claim without introducing an unsupported one?
- Is the focal point clear at the intended viewing size?
- Is essential text readable and is useful alt text available?
- Does the visual follow the selected brand's palette and tone without copying another brand?
- Is the returned URL or artifact verifiable before any publication decision?

If any input or capability is missing, begin with `Partial result:`, preserve the brief, identify the unverified check, and do not guess or retry a paid generation automatically.
